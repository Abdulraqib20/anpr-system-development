Android - IP Webcam App:
  1. Install "IP Webcam" app from Google Play Store
  2. Open the app and scroll down to click "Start server"
  3. Note the IP address shown (e.g., 192.168.0.88:8080)
  4. Your camera URL will be: http://192.168.0.88:8080/video
  5. Use the "IP Webcam" quick setup button above
iOS - EpocCam or similar:
  1. Install "EpocCam" or "iVCam" from App Store
  2. Connect your phone and computer to the same WiFi
  3. Follow the app's setup instructions
  4. Note the streaming URL provided by the app
  5. Enter the URL in the field above
Important: Ensure your phone and computer are on the same WiFi network. Update the IP address in the URL to match your phone's IP address.
